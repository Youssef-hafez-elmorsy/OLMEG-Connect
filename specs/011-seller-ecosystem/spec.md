# Spec: Seller Ecosystem

**Status**: Planned  
**Entry gate**: Analytics and fulfillment foundations must exist.

## Goal

Give sellers professional operational tools instead of only product upload screens.

## Scope

- Seller revenue dashboard.
- Product views and conversion analytics.
- Low stock alerts.
- Boosted listings plan.
- Sponsored products plan.
- Merchant OCR verification plan.
- Seller trust score.

## Exit Gate

- Sellers can understand performance and act on trustworthy metrics.
- Promotions and trust scores are backend-validated.
